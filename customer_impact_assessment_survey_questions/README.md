# Customer impact assessment survey questions

This "experiment" is basically a log for coming up with customer impact assessment survey questions.

The corresponding GitHub issue is internal and not public.

The work was borne out of a brief from company leadership: scientifically assess whether the
product enables customers to be more focused, feel more connected, get more done in less time and
achieve their goals faster, starting from a short set of assessment questions. The ask was for at
most 10 questions, put to each user in an organization roughly quarterly, testing whether the
product had genuinely improved on the status quo. (Verbatim internal quotes removed for
publication.)

Six starter questions came with the brief:
1. I feel more focused (using Convictional over Slack) (likert scale)
2. I feel more connected with my team (...) (likert scale)
3. We are more likely to achieve our goals (...) (likert scale)
4. I'm able to make decisions faster than before (...) (likert scale)
5. My decisions are higher qualify (...) (likert scale)
6. It's easier to find what I need when I need it (...) (likert scale)

So this "experiment" houses the inputs and work to come up with potential custoemr impact assessment survey questions (together with Claude).

A colleague also supplied a published workplace-wellbeing survey instrument as prior art, since some of its variables are structurally similar to what this work is trying to measure. That third-party instrument is not redistributed here.
