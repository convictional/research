Decisions without logged outcomes are sparse signal for any globally optimizing feed. The feed will look to surface goal aligned, globally optimal recommendations, which will require an 'understanding' of potential impact or effect that the recommendation would have should the user choose to execute against it.

Recommendations based off of historical user actions risks a local max, overfit to their biases and historical behaviours. We therefore want to be able to predict the outcome of a recommendation in order to surface those we think will have the most impact and effect on the user's own and organizational goals.

_**See the full write-up for the full experiment and results detail, however tldr below:**_

- Outcomes generated by deep research for 30 randomly sampled decisions over the last 18 months from Convictional were evaluated by the team based on their proximal role to the decision itself
- Evaluations collected feedback on report accuracy, and outcome metadata: the current status, the most related goal, and the impact and effect on said goal
- In total 76 ratings were collected across decisions that had at least 2 raters (13 more decisions got single ratings for 89 total received ratings)
- Means:
    - Accuracy: 4.2/5 (higher is better)
    - Status: 4.24/5 (0=not started, 5=done)
    - Effect: +0.88 (on a -2,...,2 score - higher is more helpful)
    - Impact: 3.08/5 (0=none, 5=corner-stone)


- At this sample size, no statistically significant findings were observed for:
    - Role impact: directionally deciders are harsher on accuracy and impact, but not statistically significant. Not even directional indication of difference in ratings for status and effect.
    - Decision age: age of the decision has no statistical impact on the status of the outcome
    - Status: current status of the decision has no bearing on the accuracy of the report.


- Worth following up:
    - The entropy of a decision (how much it sprawls in an org) has the strongest directional indication of statistical power for impact. There may be an experiment using entropy to rank items in a feed.
    - We can tighten some statistical tests with more evals - with a flattened, multi-goal impact rating (combined impact and effect for a signed impact score), more informed routing and better instructions
