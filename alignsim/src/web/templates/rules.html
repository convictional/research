Game Rules & Reference

Goal

Grow MRR to $210,000/mo within 48 turns while keeping churn rate below 2% and runway above 10 months.

Scoring: All scores are uncapped (1.0 = hit target). Composite = sum of scores. Pareto = min of scores (weakest goal is your bottleneck). Both primary goals and function sub-goals are scored on both dimensions.

Turn Structure

Each turn you allocate capacity units across actions. Resolution happens in this order:

  1. Hiring & Firing — Sustain active hires, cancel unsustained, process new hires, tick pending, apply arrivals
  2. Engineering — Build features, fix bugs, infrastructure (tech debt reduction)
  3. Sales — Advance pipeline customers, engagement decay, timeline tick
  4. CS — Customer health updates (bug impact, support, competitive pressure)
  5. Marketing — Log investment (effects are lagged ~10 turns)
  6. Discovery — Reveal hidden customers
  7. Ops Projects — Process improvement work + bonus lifecycle
  8. Competitive events — Competitor actions fire on scheduled turns
  9. Bug injection — New bugs appear based on tech debt level (Poisson)
  10. Financial — Revenue collection, cost deduction, runway update
  11. Metrics — Churn tracking, stage advancement, history recording

Invalid actions are dropped and their capacity is lost — bad decisions cost you.

Each sell action has a base conversion probability modified by satisfaction and engagement. Hot engagement = +30%, cold = -30%. High competitive pressure reduces probability.

Timeline (Action-Triggered): Discovered customers have no ticking clock until you engage them. The countdown starts on your first sell action targeting a customer (regardless of stage). Once active, it decrements every turn. If it hits 0, the customer resets to lead with a permanent 30% satisfaction penalty per reset (floored at 30% of original). Choose when to start each clock strategically.

Customer Satisfaction (Rubric)

Each customer has a hidden rubric with four weighted components. A deal closes only when total satisfaction ≥ 70% and all dealbreaker features are shipped.

ComponentWhat It MeasuresHow to Improve
Feature Coverage Do you have the features they need, at sufficient quality? Formula: breadth × 0.4 + depth × 0.6 (breadth = fraction of needs met, depth = avg quality score across met needs) Ship the features they need at higher quality
Price Price sensitivity (inversely correlated with customer size) Larger customers score higher automatically (0.6 to 1.0)
Product Maturity Ratio of solid/polished features to total shipped. ALL shipped features count, not just the customer's needs. Upgrade features from MVP to solid/polished. Shipping many MVPs hurts this.
Support Customer health / 10 CS support actions, fix bugs affecting the customer

Financial Model

Each turn: Revenue (MRR) minus Costs (team + overhead + maintenance) = net income added to budget.

  • Team cost: capacity/turn × $2,500 (so 40 capacity = $100,000/turn)
  • Base overhead: $10,000/turn (rent, tools, etc.)
  • Feature maintenance: Each shipped feature has a per-turn maintenance cost ($400-$900)

Runway = budget / monthly burn (when costs > revenue). If budget hits 0, the game ends in bankruptcy.
